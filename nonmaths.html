Writing, communication & public engagement

I enjoy writing about mathematics as something created by people, with a history and a wider cultural context. I am also interested in how mathematics is taught, communicated and experienced by students. Much of my work sits somewhere between those interests.

Creative writing

Alongside mathematical writing, I maintain a more personal writing practice focused on identity and transformation. I mostly write creative nonfiction, often influenced by existentialist and absurdist ideas, and I am interested in how voice and form change in response to lived experience.

Some of this work is unpublished and intentionally personal. I have also written book reviews for the University of Manchester Library Student Team and use my personal blog as a space to experiment. This writing is quite different from my mathematical work, but it is necessary in the sense that it helps me not lose my head.
